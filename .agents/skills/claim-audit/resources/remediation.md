# Remediation Strategies

Not every flagged claim requires inline revision. Match the fix to the claim's importance.

## Remediation Options

| Option | When to Use | Trade-off |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| **Inline revision** | Load-bearing claims; errors that mislead | Full accuracy; may add length |
| **Inline hedge** | Illustrative claims; quick fix sufficient | Adds few words; signals uncertainty |
| **Footnote/endnote** | Dispute is real but tangential | Preserves flow; details available |
| **Parenthetical aside** | Reader should know but needn't dwell | Slightly interrupts flow |
| **Omission** | Claim isn't needed; removing improves text | Cleanest if claim is decorative |
| **Accept imprecision** | Illustrative; conventional figure; low stakes | No change; document decision |

## Decision Tree

1. **Is the claim load-bearing?**
   - Yes → Inline revision required
   - No → Continue

2. **Is the claim wrong or seriously misleading?**
   - Yes → Inline revision or omission
   - No → Continue

3. **Would a reader be misled in a way that matters?**
   - Yes → Inline hedge or parenthetical
   - No → Continue

4. **Is there scholarly dispute a careful reader should know about?**
   - Yes → Footnote
   - No → Accept imprecision

## Worked Example

**Claim:** "The pamphlet sold roughly 500,000 copies in a population of 2.5 million."

**Audit result:** WEAKLY GROUNDED (stretched; figure from Paine's own claims, disputed by historians)

**Salience:** ILLUSTRATIVE (core argument is "extraordinary reach," not the exact number)

### Remediation Options Compared

| Option | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Inline revision | "The pamphlet achieved extraordinary circulation—Paine later claimed 500,000 copies, though modern historians estimate 75,000-150,000 printed in the first year, with additional exposure through shared copies and public readings." |
| Inline hedge | "The pamphlet sold perhaps 100,000-500,000 copies..." |
| Footnote | Keep original; add note: "Paine's claim of 500,000 is widely repeated but disputed; see Loughran (2007) for lower estimates." |
| Parenthetical | "...sold roughly 500,000 copies (a figure Paine promoted, though historians debate it)..." |
| Accept imprecision | Keep original; note in audit that the exact figure is illustrative and the core claim (extraordinary reach) holds regardless |

**Recommended:** Footnote or inline hedge. Full revision adds complexity disproportionate to the claim's importance.

## Common Hedge Phrases

- "roughly," "approximately," "about"
- "by some estimates," "reportedly"
- "as few as X or as many as Y"
- "at least," "up to"
- "according to [source]" (shifts burden)
